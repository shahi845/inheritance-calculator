# Classical References & Evidentiary Sources

This document maps the mathematical inheritance algorithms and rules implemented in the calculator to classical Islamic jurisprudence texts (*Fiqh al-Farā'iḍ*) and textual evidence (*Adillah*).

---

## 📜 Primary Scriptural Sources (Quran & Sunnah)

### 1. Surah An-Nisa (4:11) — Descendants & Parents
- **Daughters**: 1 daughter gets $1/2$; 2 or more daughters share $2/3$.
- **Parents with Descendants**: Father gets $1/6$; Mother gets $1/6$.
- **Parents without Descendants**: Mother gets $1/3$; Father takes the residue as *ʿAṣabah*.
- **Siblings effect on Mother**: 2 or more siblings reduce Mother's share from $1/3$ to $1/6$.

### 2. Surah An-Nisa (4:12) — Spouses & Uterine (Maternal) Siblings
- **Husband**: $1/2$ if no descendants; $1/4$ if descendants exist.
- **Wife / Wives**: $1/4$ if no descendants; $1/8$ if descendants exist (shared equally among up to 4 wives).
- **Maternal Siblings**: 1 maternal sibling gets $1/6$; 2 or more share $1/3$ equally (male and female share equally 1:1).

### 3. Surah An-Nisa (4:176) — Kalālah (Full & Consanguine Siblings)
- **Full / Paternal Sister**: 1 sister gets $1/2$; 2 or more share $2/3$.
- **Sister with Brother**: Full/Paternal brother makes sister a residuary in a 2:1 ratio (*ʿAṣabah bi-Ghayrih*).

## 📚 Classical Fiqh Manuscripts & References

### 1. *Matn al-Rahbiyyah* (متن الرحبية) — Ibn al-Mutqina (d. 577 AH)
- **Domain**: Didactic poem on Farā'iḍ accepted across Shāfiʿī, Ḥanbalī, and Jumhūr tradition.
- **Used for**: Fixed share conditions, blocking hierarchy (*Ḥajb*), and ʿAwl rules.

### 2. *Bidayat al-Mujtahid wa Nihayat al-Muqtasid* (بداية المجتهد) — Ibn Rushd (Averroes, d. 595 AH)
- **Domain**: Comparative Islamic Jurisprudence (*Fiqh al-Muqāran*).
- **Used for**: Modeling Madhhab differences (Al-Mushtarakah, Grandfather vs Siblings, Dhawū al-Arḥām).

### 3. *Al-Majmu' Sharh al-Muhadhdhab* (المجموع شرح المهذب) — Imam al-Nawawi (d. 676 AH)
- **Domain**: Authoritative Shāfiʿī Fiqh reference.
- **Used for**: Shāfiʿī classical vs modern fallback treasury rules (*Bayt al-Māl* vs Radd).

### 4. *Al-Fatawa al-Hindiyyah* (الفتاوى الهندية) & *Rad al-Muhtar* (رد المحتار) — Ibn Abidin (d. 1252 AH)
- **Domain**: Authoritative Ḥanafī Fiqh references.
- **Used for**: Ḥanafī 4-class Dhawū al-Arḥām system and Grandfather blocking rules.

### 5. *Al-Mughni* (المغني) — Ibn Qudamah (d. 620 AH)
- **Domain**: Authoritative Ḥanbalī Fiqh reference.
- **Used for**: Ḥanbalī *Tanzīl* (representation) method for distant kindred.

## 🏛️ Famous Named Classical Cases (*Al-Masā'il al-Mashhūrah*)

1. **Al-Gharrawiyyatayn (Al-ʿUmariyyatayn)**:
   - Case 1: Husband + Father + Mother $\to$ Husband $1/2$, Mother $1/3$ of remainder ($1/6$), Father residue ($1/3$).
   - Case 2: Wife + Father + Mother $\to$ Wife $1/4$, Mother $1/3$ of remainder ($1/4$), Father residue ($1/2$).
2. **Al-Minbariyyah**:
   - Wife + 2 Daughters + Father + Mother $\to$ Base 24 increases by ʿAwl to 27 (Wife $3/27$, Daughters $16/27$, Father $4/27$, Mother $4/27$).
3. **Al-Mushtarakah (Al-Ḥimāriyyah)**:
   - Husband + Mother + 2 Maternal Siblings + Full Brother $\to$ Shāfiʿī/Mālikī: Full brother shares the $1/3$ with maternal siblings; Ḥanafī/Ḥanbalī: Full brother receives $0$.
4. **Al-Akdariyya**:
   - Husband + Mother + Paternal Grandfather + Full Sister $\to$ Famous grandfather/sister interaction case.
