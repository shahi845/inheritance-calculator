ℹ️ About Islamic Inheritance Platform

This platform was designed as an educational tool for students, researchers, and families to learn the intricate divine laws of Islamic estate distribution (Farāʾiḍ).

Software Architecture

  • Decoupled Calculation Engine: Pure mathematical logic isolated in src/core and src/madhahib.
  • Educational Why Breakdown: Automatic step-by-step evidence extraction for every calculated heir share.
  • Multi-Madhhab Engineering: Tested implementations for Shāfiʿī, Ḥanafī, Mālikī, Ḥanbalī, and consensus Jumhūr models.
Scholar Review Disclaimer: This application is intended for educational and analytical purposes. For binding real-world estate settlements, consult a qualified Islamic scholar or local religious authority.
